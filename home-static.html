Espil Online

Espil Online is a community and a free knowledge, information and technology sharing solution, presented in the form of a game. It is a real-time digital replica of the real world - beginning with Vancouver - where you can walk the streets, visit shops, drive, and meet friends. The world keeps growing from there.
